SECTION 2 — PROJECT DETAILS
=====================================================================

Project Name:
Complaint Sathi — AI Triage for Citizen Complaints (CivicLens)

Project Description (2–3 lines):
Complaint Sathi turns an unstructured citizen complaint — free text in English, Hindi, Kannada or Hinglish, with photos — into a structured, prioritised ticket: AI suggests the category, a severity with a plain-language reason, and the right department, and the citizen confirms before anything is filed. Officers work a severity-sorted queue and resolve complaints with a note and an after-work photo the citizen can see, while every approval and status change is written to a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit log.

Successfully Implemented Features:
- Multilingual complaint submission (English, Kannada, Hindi and Hinglish).
- AI-powered complaint categorisation and severity detection.
- Explainable severity assessment with a plain-language reason.
- AI-ranked department recommendations based on ward and category.
- Duplicate complaint prevention.
- Three role-based portals: Citizen, Department Officer and Admin.
- Severity-prioritised complaint dashboards.
- Verified department registration and admin-controlled activation.
- Complaint status tracking with citizen-visible updates.
- Mandatory resolution notes and after-work photo proof.
- Tamper-evident, digitally signed audit trails.
- Database-enforced access control and business rules.
- Automatic reassignment of open complaints when a department is suspended.
- Admin analytics for complaints, departments and Bengaluru wards.
- AI fallback classification when the AI service is unavailable.
- Audit verification and exportable signed evidence.

Additional Features Developed:
- In-app notifications for citizens and officers on every status change.
- Voice-based complaint registration: tap the mic and speak in English, Kannada or Hindi — the speech fills the complaint form and the AI auto-fills category, severity and department for confirmation.
- Optional Firebase email verification on registration (add-on; the main email + password login flow is untouched).
